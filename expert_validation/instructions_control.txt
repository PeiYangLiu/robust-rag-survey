Control-condition instructions

You will judge whether pairs of robust-RAG papers can be directly compared.
Use your own expert criteria. Do not use the five-coordinate protocol unless it
was separately provided to you by the study administrator.

For each pair, choose one verdict:

- valid: the two papers can be directly compared as substitutes for the same
  robustness question.
- partial: the papers are related but compare only as complementary components,
  settings, or partial overlaps.
- invalid: the papers answer different questions or define incompatible
  assumptions, so a direct ranking would be misleading.
- under-specified: the available paper descriptions omit information needed to
  decide.

For each decision, record:

1. verdict
2. confidence from 1 to 5
3. decision time in seconds
4. a short rationale
5. optional error type if you noticed a missing or ambiguous assumption

Please do not discuss the task with other participants during annotation.
