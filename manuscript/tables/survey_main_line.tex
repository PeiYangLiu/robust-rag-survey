Evidence construction & What enters the context? & Missing positives, irrelevant passages, hard negatives, stale evidence, insufficient contexts. & Retrieval is context construction, not a neutral recall front end; robustness depends on rank, hardness, support availability, and downstream utility. \\
Evidence use & How does the generator use evidence? & Ignored support, over-trusted distractors, unsupported citations, parametric-vs-retrieved conflict. & Context availability is not context use; robust RAG needs claim-level support, source reliability, and conflict-aware reasoning. \\
Adversarial integrity & Can the evidence channel be trusted? & Corpus poisoning, retriever manipulation, indirect prompt injection, backdoors, privacy extraction. & Security claims transfer only inside a threat model; attack success must be normalised by attacker capability and defense action. \\
System action control & What should the system do under uncertainty? & Unanswerability, insufficient evidence, calibration error, overconfident unsupported answers. & Robustness requires action policies--answer, abstain, retrieve again, verify, clarify, expose conflict, or escalate--not confidence scores alone. \\
Evaluation & What should benchmarks reward? & Accuracy-only rankings, faithfulness-only scores, attack-only metrics, missing utility/cost reporting. & Evaluation should align evidence state, metric target, action policy, and cost; otherwise leaderboard results are not comparable. \\
Extension layer & How do core evidence failures change when evidence is not a text passage? & Cross-modal misalignment, visual/layout evidence loss, graph-structure distortion, table-scale retrieval, agentic search drift, domain-specific safety risk. & Multimodal, graph, table, agentic, tool, code, and domain RAG are retained as stress tests of typed evidence states and actions, not as a full co-equal survey scope. \\
