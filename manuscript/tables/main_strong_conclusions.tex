More retrieved context is not monotonically more robust. & IrrelCtx, RAAT, RECOMP, RankRAG, and RGB show that rank, negative type, compression, and generator utility change outcomes \citep{irrelevantContext2023,raat2024,recomp2024,venueExtra014,rgb2023}. & Larger or longer contexts can help when the bottleneck is missing gold evidence. & Must state gold-evidence availability, distractor hardness, rank position, and context budget. & Report context-construction grids, not only top-$k$ recall or answer accuracy. \\
Evidence availability is different from evidence use. & RAGTruth, FaithEval, Trust-Align, and Lost-in-the-Middle show unsupported spans, context-faithfulness failures, refusal/attribution tradeoffs, and position sensitivity \citep{ragTruth2024,rrag040,venueAcc001,liu2024lostmiddle}. & Short single-hop questions with explicit support may be solved by simple retrieval. & Depends on support granularity, context position, source conflict, and citation unit. & Evaluate claim-level support and source use, not document-level citation alone. \\
Knowledge conflict is a source-reliability problem, not a choice between retrieval and memory. & FaithfulRAG, AstuteRAG, ParamMute, and reconciliation work probe conflicts between parametric and retrieved evidence \citep{faithfulRAG2025,astuteRAG2025,rrag024,venueAcc027}. & In a fresh authoritative corpus, trusting retrieval may be a safe default. & Requires source freshness, provenance, authority, and adversary assumptions. & Benchmarks should label source disagreement and the required action: reconcile, expose, defer, or answer. \\
Natural conflict and poisoning are different scientific games. & Conflict papers and poisoning/security papers use different assumptions even when both contain contradictory text \citep{faithfulRAG2025,astuteRAG2025,poisonedRAG2024,rrag021,rrag017}. & Both can share claim/source annotation schemas. & Poisoning requires attacker access, insertion budget, query control, and retrieved-malicious-evidence status. & Do not transfer conflict-resolution results to security defenses without threat-model normalization. \\
RAG security progress is role-specific. & Venue-linked security work covers corpus poisoning, memory/agent poisoning, data extraction, and robust filtering/certification-style defenses \citep{poisonedRAG2024,rrag053,venue200SpillBeans,rrag021,rrag017}. & A shared attack-defense harness can rank methods inside one threat model. & Prevention, demotion, detection, traceback, recovery, privacy extraction, and certification are different outcomes. & Security papers should report capability envelope, clean utility, and defense action, not only attack success. \\
Calibration is not an action policy. & Self-RAG, FLARE/DRAGIN-style active retrieval, CalibRAG, and query-knowledge relevance work separate retrieval need, confidence, and decision reliability \citep{selfRAG2023,flare2023active,venue200DRAGIN,calibRAG2025,venue200QueryKnowledge}. & In low-risk answer-only settings, calibrated confidence may be sufficient. & Must state confidence target, available actions, coverage, latency, and utility/cost. & Report selective risk by evidence sufficiency and action, not only ECE or confidence. \\
Robust-RAG benchmarks are diagnostic slices, not one scale. & RGB, RAGTruth, RAGAS, ARES, BEIR, BERGEN, and RAGLAB measure different objects: abilities, support labels, LLM-judge metrics, retrieval transfer, and tooling \citep{rgb2023,ragTruth2024,ragas2024,ares2024,beir2021,venue200BERGEN,venue200RAGLAB}. & Methods evaluated in the same harness and action set can be ranked. & Need evidence-state labels, metric target, action policy, cost, and threat model. & Replace universal robustness leaderboards with diagnostic scorecards. \\
Beyond-text RAG is best used here as typed-evidence stress tests. & ColPali, MMed-RAG, TableRAG, and graph RAG show that images, pages, tables, and graph neighborhoods have validity conditions unlike passages \citep{colpali2025,mmedrag2025,tableRAG2024,venueAcc004}. & OCR-only or passage-extracted settings may reduce to ordinary text RAG. & Need modality-specific grounding, provenance, schema, visual region, graph edge, or tool-memory labels. & Treat extension papers as stress tests for the comparability lens unless doing a separate systematic multimodal/agentic survey. \\
