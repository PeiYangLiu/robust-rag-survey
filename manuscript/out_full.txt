Robust retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
papers often share labels such as noise robustness, faithfulness, security, or calibration while
evaluating different evidence conditions, interventions, outcomes, and actions. Using a
536-paper scoping corpus and a 342-row evidence map as documentary context, we synthesize the field through five operating questions: context construction, evidence use, adversarial integrity, action control, and evaluation. We then ask what experiments designed
to audit cross-paper claims actually estimate.
Our estimand audit separates assignment, item,
reference, and downstream decision units. A
planned 24-auditor, 480-claim collection is excluded because no independently verifiable participant package exists; none of its outcomes
or participant attributes is reported. In 5,000
null simulations of its design, a source-only
test rejects 16.3% of the time with moderate
rater heterogeneity, versus 4.0% for participant
randomization inference; doubling the sources
raises source-only rejection to 27.2% while the
number of randomized people remains fixed.
An earlier 80-claim diagnostic also shows that
the proposed five-field checklist is not a validated instrument. We release the survey map,
simulation, exclusion trail, and a consequencedefined counterfactual target. More claims cannot replace randomized reviewers, and agreement with a prompt-overlapping reference is
not independent validity.

Retrieval quality is often summarized by recall, 143
but robust generation depends on the context that 144
reaches the model. Irrelevant passages can redi- 145
rect an answer even when supporting evidence is 146
also present (Yoran et al., 2024); random nega- 147
tives, hard negatives, counterfactual passages, and 148
missing evidence create different operating con- 149
ditions (Fang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a). 150
Consequently, retrieval-side filtering and rerank- 151
ing, context compression, noise-aware training, and 152
generator-side evidence reading address related but 153
non-substitutable problems (Yu et al., 2023; Wang 154
et al., 2023; Trappolini et al., 2025). Long-context 155
access does not remove this distinction: adding 156
passages can improve evidence availability while 157
simultaneously making selection and attribution 158
harder (Gao et al., 2026; Jin et al., 2024a).
159
Sufficiency adds a second boundary. When sup- 160
port is absent, the appropriate response may be to 161
retrieve again or abstain rather than to become more 162
robust to distractors (Joren et al., 2025). Prove- 163
nance and freshness matter for the same reason: a 164
topically relevant passage can still be stale, low- 165
quality, or inappropriate for the decision. Thus, 166
“better retrieval” should identify the evidence state 167
being improved and the downstream behavior it 168
is meant to enable, rather than collapse context 169
construction into a single recall number.

Limitations

The simulation uses binary correctness, a common
wrong-label state, balanced panels of three among
563 six reviewers, and chosen variance parameters. It
564 demonstrates a design failure mode rather than es565 timating its prevalence. Exact randomization infer566 ence is evaluated under the simulated sharp null;
567 a real study would need an estimand and interval
568 appropriate to its assignment mechanism.
569
The 80-claim diagnostic is purposive, overlaps
570 development, has only three coders and one adjudi571 cator, and lacks a recoverable selection procedure
572 for 64 items. It does not validate the checklist.
573 The broader corpus is open-PDF based, model574 assisted, incomplete as a census, and unsuitable
575 for prevalence claims. The controlled benchmark
576 is synthetic and has no reported judge outcomes.
577
The 480-claim collection has no verifiable
578 independent-participant package and is excluded
579 completely. This removes the manuscript’s former
580 empirical prompt contrast rather than repairing it.
581 Local hashes are not external preregistration. No
582 consequence-based human effect size or equiva583 lence margin is estimated.

What Changes Under an Attacker?

Corpus poisoning makes evidence unreliability intentional. PoisonedRAG inserts passages to steer
1144 target answers; earlier corpus-poisoning work ma1145 nipulates retrieval through adversarial passages
1146 (Zou et al., 2025; Zhong et al., 2023). Attack suc1147 cess is conditional on corpus write access, insertion
1148 budget, query knowledge, retriever visibility, and
1149 target scope. Omitting these fields can make two
1150 attack rates numerically similar but scientifically
1151 incomparable.
1152
Indirect prompt injection and backdoors target
1153 a different interface. Retrieved text may contain
1154 instructions that override the user task, or a trig1155 ger may activate behavior learned during training
1156 (Chaturvedi et al., 2025; Chaudhari et al., 2024;
1157 Cheng et al., 2024). These attacks should not in1158 herit claims from factual poisoning merely because
1159 both place malicious text in the context. Retrieval
1160 realism, instruction-following behavior, and trigger
1161 access define distinct games.
1162
Defenses likewise have different roles. Certified
1163 RAG gives guarantees under a bounded corruption
1164 model; filtering and semantic isolation demote sus1165 picious documents; traceback identifies likely poi1166 soned sources (Xiang et al., 2024; Si et al., 2025;
1167 Zhang et al., 2025a). Prevention, certified accu1168 racy, detection, and recovery require different out1169 comes. Any cross-defense comparison should pair
1170 the same capability envelope with clean utility and
1171 state whether the defense prevents, detects, traces,
1172 quarantines, or recovers.
