# Expanded expert-validation analysis plan

This packet is not completed validation evidence. It defines a 60-pair stratified candidate pool and templates for collecting annotator expertise, participant judgments, and multiple independent gold adjudications.

Planned reports after data collection:
1. Pair-stratum coverage and annotator expertise distribution.
2. Inter-gold agreement before panel adjudication.
3. Control vs protocol agreement with adjudicated gold.
4. Clustered bootstrap by annotator and pair.
5. Mixed-effects logistic model with random intercepts for annotator and pair when sample size permits.
6. Disagreement taxonomy by failure family and comparison coordinate.
