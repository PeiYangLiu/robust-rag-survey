# Systematic Literature Protocol, Coverage-Bias Audit, and Taxonomic Coding Rules

## 1. Search Scope, Platforms, and Coverage-Bias Remediation
- **Time Window**: May 2020 (following Lewis et al., 2020) through June 2026 (core freeze), plus a targeted post-freeze pass through October 2026 governed by Rules S1–S3.
- **Indexed Digital Libraries & Venues**:
  - **ACL Anthology**: Full bibliography scan of 12,924 entries (2020–2026) across ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, TACL, COLING, and Findings.
  - **OpenReview / ML & Security / IR Venues**: 15 DBLP and 15 OpenAlex structured API queries (top-200 cap) paired with direct proceedings sweeps across ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, USENIX Security, IEEE S&P, ACM CCS, SIGIR, WWW, KDD, CIKM, WSDM, and AAAI.
  - **arXiv Preprint Queries**: 55 Boolean queries (`cs.CL`, `cs.IR`, `cs.CR`, `cs.LG`, `cs.AI`), `submittedDate`-ordered (capped at 80–320 hits per query, yielding 1,904 raw records).
- **Quantifying and Remediating Search Truncation, Accessibility, and Selection Biases**:
  1. **Disposition of the 459 Initial No-Open-PDF / Parse-Error Hits**: Among the 1,904 initial Stage-1a hits, 1,445 had directly downloadable open PDFs and 459 lacked open PDFs or failed PDF parsing (primarily paywalled IEEE/ACM/Springer cross-listings or withdrawn metadata stubs). During Stage 1b/1c venue sweeps and cross-repository linking, 312 of those 459 records were matched to open arXiv or ACL/OpenReview/PMLR proceedings PDFs, and 143 were excluded as out-of-scope non-RAG papers or broken stubs, leaving only **4 paywalled ACM records** (`venue044, venue045, venue049, venue050`) retained as metadata/abstract-verified entries in the 330 core primary RAG studies (**326/330 = 98.79% full-text PDF verified; residual paywall rate = 1.21%**).
  2. **Boundary Between Systematic Inclusion and Representative Selection (Rules R1–R3) & Unified-Scope Re-Audit of Round-2 Exclusions**:
     - Out of 1,399 deduplicated Stage-1a records, 574 off-topic and 289 general-background records were excluded (documented at the aggregate funnel level in `corpus_snapshot.json`, whereas `coverage_decisions.csv` logs the 476 post-triage inclusion, duplicate-merge, and Stage-1b/1c adjudication records), leaving 536 broadly relevant candidates: 250 entered the base evidence Map-A, 50 served as foundational/narrative anchors, and 236 were deprioritized during initial triage (**167 scope exclusions** evaluating only clean-context domain deployment without an $\Omega$ stressor vs. **69 representative-selection exclusions**).
     - Furthermore, all 6 historical Round-2 exclusions in `coverage_decisions.csv` were re-audited under the **unified multi-modality/code/graph/agentic $\Omega$ scope standard**, eliminating legacy `outside_text_scope` tags: `venue001` (Glass et al., EMNLP 2021) is consolidated as a cross-stratum duplicate of `venueExpanded059` (12 duplicate merges total), while code-RAG attacks `rrag194` (`ImportSnare`) and `rrag196` (`VenomRACG`) are recognized as in-scope under $\Omega_{\text{adv}}$ and classified under **Rule R1** as secondary variants of the RACG poisoning/backdoor family anchored by `rrag197` (Bo Lin et al., 2025) and `trojanRAG2024` / `badRAG2024`.
     - Representative selection is governed by three explicit rules: **Rule R1 (Same Strand & C1–C5 Cell)** excludes secondary incremental variants occupying the same `(Pillar, Strand, C1–C5)` cell as an included canonical anchor; **Rule R2 (Mandatory Retention of Negative Results & Counterexamples)** mandates retaining any study reporting negative results, failure boundaries, or empirical counterexamples (**0 counterexamples excluded across all 69 + 2 = 71 excluded secondary variants**); and **Rule R3 (Denominator Eligibility)** separates **mechanistic synthesis over canonical representatives** ($N = 330$ pre-June core and $N = 355$ total cited primary studies) from **frequency analysis over the full eligible study pool** ($N = 401 = 330 + 71$ pre-June core eligible and $N = 426 = 355 + 71$ total eligible studies, archived row by row in `data/secondary_variants_71_sensitivity.csv` and `.json`, with maximum row-computed drift $2.77 \le 3.45$ percentage points).
     - To prevent circularity from restricting the June–October 2026 update ($25$ primary studies) to the $20$ strands, all structural coverage observations are verified separately on the unconstrained Stage-1 pre-June pools ($N = 330$ and $N = 401$), across strand-independent publication-year and venue strata, and alongside an **open-coding residual audit** (`data/open_coding_and_temporal_audit.json`) logging cross-cutting mechanisms (`parametric_memory_editing_vs_rag`, `test_time_long_cot_search_scaling`, `cryptographic_or_tee_index_attestation`, and `schema_constrained_query_compilation`). All reported percentages (`264/355 = 74.4%`, `5/355 = 1.4%`, `0/355 = 0.0%`) are descriptive sample statistics of the audited corpus under our coding protocol and Definition~1 (where `0/355` means no benchmark satisfies Definition~1's complete coupled specification across Properties~1–3 under a shared provenance-tiered $W_{\mathcal{T}}^{\text{ref}}$ and budget $B$, while multi-slice suites such as RGB, CUB, FaithEval, SafeRAG, and MIRAGE evaluate pairwise capability combinations).
  3. **Post-Inclusion Stability vs. External Recall Boundary & Explicit Temporal Year Definitions**: Adding 54 foundational, venue-expanded, and late-breaking works (governed by Rules S1–S3 during the June–October 2026 post-freeze pass) raises pre-2024 representation from `14/336 = 4.17%` in the core map to **`38/390 = 9.74%`** across all cited works by first-release year (`year_in_map` in `citation_index.csv`: `14` core + `24` supplementary), or from `9/336 = 2.68%` to **`33/390 = 8.46%`** by final conference/journal publication year in `references.bib` (where 5 late-2023 arXiv preprints `chainOfNote2023`, `irrelevantContext2023`, `rrag043`, `rrag226`, and `xie2024chameleon` appeared in 2024 proceedings). Across the nested `326` full-text core primary studies, `330` core primary studies, and `355` cited primary RAG studies, C1–C5 coordinate shares drift by at most **`2.13` percentage points**. We explicitly distinguish this nested-sample stability from external retrieval recall: because `submittedDate` arXiv caps (80–320 hits) and top-200 API limits can miss unindexed pre-2024 or long-tail works, our corpus is presented as a protocol-documented, multi-source scoping synthesis rather than an exhaustive population census.

## 2. Canonical Data Architecture and Source-Located C1–C5 Semantic Audit
1. **Single Canonical Source (`data/literature_map.csv`)**:
   - `data/literature_map.csv` (336 core records) and the 54 supplementary records in `scripts/sync_canonical_data.py` form the single canonical source of truth. Running `python3 survey_reproducibility/scripts/sync_canonical_data.py` deterministically regenerates `data/citation_index.csv` (390 rows) and `data/coverage_decisions.csv` (476 rows), guaranteeing **0 cross-file discrepancies** across all shared fields.
2. **Primary (`C1_evidence_state`), Secondary (`C1_secondary_regimes`), and Complete Union (`C1_all_applicable`)**:
   - Across all 330 core primary studies (with 6 core foundational/survey records including BEIR `venueExpanded087` marked `N/A_foundational_or_survey`), the three C1 columns satisfy the strict set-partition invariant:
     $$\texttt{set(C1\_all\_applicable)} = \{\texttt{C1\_evidence\_state}\} \cup \texttt{set(C1\_secondary\_regimes)}, \quad \texttt{C1\_evidence\_state} \notin \texttt{set(C1\_secondary\_regimes)}.$$
   - **Two-Step Primary Assignment Rule**:
     - *Step 1 (Strand-Anchored Primary Regime)*: A study's primary `C1_evidence_state` is anchored to the primary perturbation regime of its core contribution and taxonomic strand (`Strand 3.x` $\to$ `adversarially_manipulated`; `Strand 2.2–2.4` $\to$ `conflicting_evidence`; `Strand 1.1–1.4, 2.1` $\to$ `noisy_or_distracting`; `Strand 4.1–4.5` $\to$ `insufficient_or_uncertain`; general-purpose cross-pipeline/domain evaluation harnesses $\to$ `clean_or_heterogeneous_mix`), while `C1_secondary_regimes` records auxiliary evaluation splits (e.g., `raat2024` and `venueExpanded148` target `noisy_or_distracting` as primary and evaluate `conflicting_evidence` as secondary; `rrag001` URAG and `rrag007` NAACL target `insufficient_or_uncertain` calibration as primary and evaluate under `noisy_or_distracting` retrieval).
     - *Step 2 (Severity-Precedence Tie-Breaker for Multi-Stressor Benchmarks)*: For diagnostic benchmarks spanning multiple stressors without a single method locus (`rrag019` SafeRAG, `rrag032` CUB, `rrag040` FaithEval, `rrag070` Synthetic Web, `rrag202` URAQ, `venue006` NPOV, `venue021` BordIRlines, `venue024` RAG-RewardBench), `C1_evidence_state` is adjudicated by the severity hierarchy `adversarially_manipulated > conflicting_evidence > noisy_or_distracting > insufficient_or_uncertain > clean_or_heterogeneous_mix`.
   - Under this protocol, **41 multi-regime core studies** have a secondary evaluation split in `C1_secondary_regimes` whose severity rank exceeds their strand-anchored primary `C1_evidence_state`; both the strand-anchored primary distribution, the unconditional severity-precedence distribution over `C1_all_applicable`, and the full multi-label coverage counts are reported in `data/corpus_snapshot.json`.
3. **Four-Level Validation: Search Coverage, Screening Agreement, Stratified Final-Coordinate Recoding, and Alternative-Rule Sensitivity**:
   - Upstream double-coding reliability ($\kappa = 0.853$ on Map-A inclusion; 148-study Map-B blind second-coding $\kappa = 0.769$ on inclusion, $0.719$ on primary failure type, $0.762$ on method family, and $0.724$ on intervention point, where dual human coders jointly overturned $18.2\%$ to $48.6\%$ of initial heuristic labels) measures pre-consensus screening agreement, **not** independent human double-coding of C1–C5.
   - To validate the final C1–C5 coordinates without conflating file synchronization with semantic validity, we pair a **five-stratum full-table source audit** across all $355$ primary studies with an archived **independent stratified protocol-to-coordinate recoding audit** (`data/stratified_80_protocol_recoding_audit.csv` and `.json`: $N = 80$ primary studies across all $20$ strands, $4$ per strand, released with dual-coder raw labels and full confusion matrices, achieving $1.000$ exact set-match on `C1_all_applicable`, $\kappa = 0.842$ on primary C1, $0.964$ on modality, $0.892$ on C2, $0.910$ on C3, $0.928$ on C4, and $0.956$ on C5) and a **four-rule sensitivity audit** (comparing Strand-Anchored, Severity-Precedence, Benign-Split-First, and Uncollapsed Multi-Label Union rules across $N \in \{326, 330, 355, 401, 426\}$):
     - **BEIR (`venueExpanded087`)**: Reclassified from `primary_rag_study` (`end_to_end_task_em_and_f1` / `single_pass_grounded_answering`) to `foundational_or_survey` (foundational zero-shot retrieval benchmark) with `C3 = evaluation_protocol_and_benchmark`, `C4 = retrieval_ranking_and_exposure` (`nDCG@10`, `Recall@100`), and `C5 = N/A_retrieval_only_benchmark` (alongside retrieval-only primary studies `rrag168` SciRerankBench, `poisoningRetrievalCorpora2023`, `rrag090`, and `colpali2025`, which account for the `4/355 = 1.1%` [`3/330 = 0.9%` core] `N/A_retrieval_only_benchmark` entries in C5 so `264 + 22 + 18 + 25 + 22 + 4 = 355` and `246 + 22 + 17 + 22 + 20 + 3 = 330`).
     - **URAG (`rrag001`) & Conformal/UQ vs. Abstention Disambiguation**: Reclassified URAG from `closed_loop_system_control` / `selective_abstention_or_clarification` to `C3 = evaluation_protocol_and_benchmark` and a dedicated C5 category `conformal_set_or_uncertainty_calibration` (which separates the 17 core + 1 supplementary conformal-set and uncertainty/calibration scoring studies from the 22 core studies that execute closed-loop abstention or clarification).
     - **Benchmark & Forensic Locus Alignments**: Updated 12 C3 records (aligning benchmarks `rrag001`, `rrag019`, `rrag114`, `rrag142`, `rrag148`, `rrag157`, `rrag164`, `rrag168`, `rrag190`, `venueExpanded088` to `evaluation_protocol_and_benchmark`, `rrag178` to `generator_reading_and_representations`, and forensic traceback `rrag208` AttnTrace to `corpus_and_index_security` / `multi_pass_verification_or_aggregation`), alongside 26 C1 reclassifications, 15 C2 reconciliations, 11 C4 metric alignments, and 14 populated metric fields, enforced by automated semantic assertions in `verify_survey_artifacts.py`.

## 3. Complementary Nature of C1–C5 and Boundary of Adjacent Transferable Works
- **Complementary Reporting Dimensions (C1–C5)**: The four taxonomic pillars couple architectural intervention locus (C3) with adversarial vs. non-adversarial origin (C1/C2). Similarly, C1 (`adversarially_manipulated`) and C2 (attacker access and insertion budget $m$) are structurally coupled on security studies; we therefore designate C1–C5 as **five complementary reporting dimensions** rather than strictly orthogonal axes.
- **Separation of Adjacent Transferable Works**: Foundational or adjacent reasoning/calibration works (`liu2026contrasts` CRPS on MCTS reasoning contrasts, `liu2026neurosym` NeuroSym-Cal on code calibration, `liu2021quadruplet` QuadrupletBERT, etc.) are explicitly tagged as `foundational_or_survey` (`N/A` on C1–C5) and separated from empirically validated RAG mechanisms in all tables and quantitative syntheses.
